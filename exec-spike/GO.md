## S12b

**Root cause.** The `srai` defect was in the Sail compiler, not in sail-riscv or our patches:
Sail 0.20.1's `lib/vector.sail` binds `sail_arith_shiftright` to `BitVec.rotateRight` for the
Lean backend (fixed upstream in rems-project/sail 895028208155, released in 0.20.2). The fix is
that hunk as `sail/00-sail-lean-asr.patch`, applied to the compiler in `nix/proof.nix`
(`zesuSailCompiler`); the model is regenerated by the pipeline, not hand-edited.

**Regenerated.** `sail-riscv-lean` now also compiles `Zba;Zbb;Zbs;Zbkb` (same profile
configuration; Zbb/Zbs are enabled through `misa.B`, which `Configured` now fixes).
`zesuSailRiscvLeanRegenerationCheck` reproduces the committed tree. Re-proofs needed: only
`maxRecDepth` for the deeper decoder chain; every existing `*_sail` and compliance statement
builds unchanged.

**Proved.** 9 forms, each with decoder, executor, soundness and Sail simulation cases (55
`Instr` constructors): `srai rev8 maxu pack packh bext bset bclri clz`. The S12 counterexample
is now refuted by `decide` (`srai 1, 1 = 0`). No open form goals remain (`OPEN/forms/`).

**Measured.** Target functions blocked 36 → 30 (any blocker 37 → 31; blocked only by non-CSR
forms 6 → 0; functions with an unsupported-form blocker 24 → 16, all CSR). Whole map blocked
135 → 122. (The committed `static.json` was stale, built with pre-S12 `FORMS`; both numbers
are from a forced `zmap build`.) Full `lake build` 12–15 min, `tools/verify.sh` passes,
three-way difftest agrees on 80/80 snapshots.

**Cost (ledger).** Regeneration 22 min (8 of them the nix build); `srai` 18 min; the eight
bit-manipulation forms 19 min together (one shared file; the other seven items carry no time).

**Forecast implication.** A regenerated model is cheap (one nix build, no proof breakage beyond
a recursion limit). Form blockers for the target are now only CSR reads.

## S17

Map precision, no proofs. Python in `tools/zmap/precision.py` (hooks in `analyze.py`), tests in
`tools/zmap/tests/test_precision.py`; `map/static.json` regenerated (`zmap build`, 40 s wall).

**Method.** One forward abstract interpretation per function computes, at every indirect call
and jump, a term for the target address (the backward slice of the pointer source, field
`slice`). Values: constants, small constant sets, entry arguments, frame offsets, loads,
bounded index ranges (byte/halfword loads, low-bit masks, `bltu`/`bgeu` against a constant).
Frame slots are tracked; a call marks unknown the slots its callee may write through a pointer
argument (per-function write summaries: argument and offset window, least fixpoint, three
rounds); `memcpy`/`memmove`/`memset` copy or fill frame slots; a CSR instruction writes through
its operand. Assumptions are listed in the module docstring (map, not proof).

**Sites by resolution kind** (the 458 indirect call sites of the S0–S8 target set):

| precondition | 392 | field chain from an argument; targets from callers: 348 have targets, 296 complete, 44 none |
| unknown | 17 | value merged from paths or computed (`deinit`s, `executeIterative`, `_zisk_main`, …) |
